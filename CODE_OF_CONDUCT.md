# Community code of conduct

Blueprints is committed to a respectful, technically rigorous, and welcoming community.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and assume good intent while evaluating evidence.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Make room for different experience levels and communication styles.
- Protect private information and handle security reports discreetly.
- Accept maintainer decisions on scope while remaining free to disagree constructively.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method on their GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock conversations, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled as privately and promptly as practical.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
